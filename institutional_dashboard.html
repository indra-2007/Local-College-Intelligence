What is the condonation procedure for attendance between 60% and 74%?

Verified

Students with 60.0% to 74.9% aggregate attendance due to genuine medical or university-authorized activities follow this process:

  • Submit valid medical documentation to the HOD within 3 working days of returning to campus.
  • Obtain endorsement from the Department Attendance Review Committee (DARC).
  • Pay the standard scrutiny fee (₹1,000 per subject) via the student portal before SEE hall ticket release.
  • Attendance strictly below 60.0% results in a Course Withdrawal (W-Grade).

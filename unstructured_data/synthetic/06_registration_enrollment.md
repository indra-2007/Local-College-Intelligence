## 2. Registration Process

### 2.1 Step-by-Step Registration
1. Log in to the Student Portal using your USN and password.
2. Navigate to "Course Registration" under the Academics tab.
3. Your mandatory courses (core subjects) are pre-filled based on your semester and branch.
4. Select your elective courses from the available list. Each elective shows the instructor name, schedule, and available seats.
5. Select your lab sections (based on available slots — first come, first served).
6. Review your complete timetable for conflicts. The system will flag any overlapping slots.
7. Click "Confirm Registration" — this action is final after the registration window closes.
8. Download and save your registration confirmation receipt.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 2.2 Credit Load Requirements
- Minimum credits per semester: 18 credits
- Maximum credits per semester: 26 credits
- Students with CGPA ≥ 8.5 may register for up to 30 credits with HoD approval.
- Students on academic probation (CGPA < 5.0) are restricted to 20 credits maximum.
- Audit courses do not count toward the credit limit but must be registered.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 2.3 Elective Selection Rules
- Open electives: Students must choose from departments other than their own.
- Professional electives: Offered within the department; minimum class size of 15 for the course to run.
- If an elective does not meet minimum enrollment, students will be reassigned to their 2nd or 3rd choice.
- Elective preference forms must be submitted during the registration window only.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

## 4. Course Withdrawal and Re-registration

### 4.1 Course Withdrawal
- Withdrawal before the penalty-free deadline: Course is removed entirely from the transcript.
- Withdrawal after the penalty-free deadline but before the "W" grade deadline: A "W" (Withdrawn) grade appears on the transcript. This does not affect CGPA.
- Withdrawal after the "W" deadline: Not permitted. Student must complete the course or receive an "F" grade.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 4.2 Re-registration for Failed Courses
- Students who fail a core course must re-register in the next available semester when the course is offered.
- Re-registration for electives: Students may either re-register for the same elective or choose an alternative (subject to availability).
- Maximum 3 attempts per course. After the 3rd failure, the case is referred to the Academic Council for review.
- Re-registered courses are charged ₹3,000 per course per attempt.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

## 5. Special Registration Categories

### 5.1 Lateral Entry Students (3rd Semester Direct Admission)
- Must complete bridge courses in Mathematics and Physics within the first year of joining.
- Bridge course credits do not count toward the degree total.
- Registration for bridge courses is automatic and mandatory.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 5.2 Students Returning from Academic Break
- Must apply for re-admission through the Registrar's Office at least 30 days before the semester begins.
- Subject to availability of seats and payment of any pending dues.
- Course mapping for returning students is done by the respective HoD.

- access: faculty, hod, admin

### 5.3 Credit Transfer (Exchange / Transfer Students)
- Credits earned at recognised institutions may be transferred with a minimum grade of "B" (or equivalent).
- Maximum 20% of total degree credits may be transferred.
- Transfer requests must include official transcripts, course syllabi, and a credit equivalence form signed by the HoD.

- access: faculty, hod, admin

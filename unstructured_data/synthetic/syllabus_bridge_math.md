                                    Unit - I                                             10    Hours
 Multivariable Calculus:Partial Differentiation: Introduction, simple problems. Total deriva-
 tive, composite functions. Jacobians – simple problems.Vector Differentiation: Introduction,
 velocity and acceleration, gradient, divergence – solenoidal vector function, curl – irrotational vector
 function and Laplacian, simple problems
                                    Unit - II                                            10    Hours
 Differential Equations:Higher order linear differential equations with constant coefficients, solu-
 tion of homogeneous equations- Complementary functions. Non-homogeneous equations – Inverse
 differential operator method of finding particular integral based on input function (force function).
                                   Unit - III                                            10    Hours
 Numerical Methods:Solution of algebraic and transcendental equations – Intermediate value prop-
 erty, Secant method, Newton-Raphson method. Solution of first order ordinary differential equations
 – Taylor series and 4th order Runge-Kutta methods. Numerical integration – Simpson’s 1/3rd, 3/8th
 and Weddle’s rules. (All methods without proof).
  Course Outcomes: After completing the course, the students will be able to:
 CO1: Explain and outline the fundamental concepts of partial differentiation, vector calculus, higher
   order linear differential equations and basic numerical methods used in engineering mathematics.
 CO2: Apply and compute solutions using multivariable calculus, vector differentiation, differential
   equation techniques, numerical algorithms to solve elementary engineering and scientific problems.
 CO3: Analyze and evaluate solutions obtained through differential calculus, vector operations, dif-
   ferential equations, numerical methods to assess their accuracy and applicability in practical
   situations.
 CO4: Integrate and summarize the knowledge of calculus, vector methods, differential equations, nu-
   merical techniques to build mathematical competence and support life-long learning in engineering
   studies.

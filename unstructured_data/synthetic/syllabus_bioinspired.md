                                    Unit - I                                             9   Hours
 Foundations of Bioinspired Engineering
 Biomimetics, nature inspired Technologies: Biological engineering principle, Termites; Sustain-
 able buildings, Insect foot adaptations for adhesion, Birds/Insects- flights/ aerodynamics, Mosquito
 inspired micro needle. Bees and Honeycomb Structure. Velcro: Hook and Lever mechanism, King-
 fisher beak - Bullet train, Whale fins - Turbine blades.
                                    Unit - II                                            9   Hours
 Nature Inspired Engineering
 Plant Inspired Technologies: Photosynthesis and Photovoltaic cells, Bionic/Artificial leaf. Lotus
 leaf effect: nature against nature: repellency against adhesion-bio-inspired nanostructures. Robotic
 Solutions Inspired by Plant Root. Namib Desert Beetle; Harvesting desert fog- Nature’s water filter.
                                   Unit - III                                            9   Hours
 Integrated Bioinspired Systems
 Bio-Inspired driven technologies for industrial applications: Biosensors: Artificial tongue
 and nose. Biomimetic echolation. Thermal insulation and storage materials. Polymer-reinforced
 and ceramic-toughened composites, lightweight biological and bioinspired materials, bio-functional
 interface, Bio-robotics.
                                   Unit - IV                                             9   Hours
 Bioinspired Computation and Intelligence
 Bio-inspired computing: Neural networks, evolutionary computing, swarm intelligence, artificial
 life, and complex networks. Bio-sensing Genetic Algorithms, Artificial Neural Networks. lab-on-chip
 devices, Artificial intelligence and MEMS.
                                    Unit - V                                             9   Hours
 Bioinspired Systems and Applications
 Machine, material and Manufacturing: Biopolymers, Bio-steel, Bio- composites, multi-
 functional biological materials. Bone-inspired lattices, nacre, spider silk, and self-healing materials.
 Antireflection and photo-thermal biomaterials, Invasive and non-invasive thermal detection inspired
 by skin. Medical Technologies: Organ system- Circulatory- artificial blood, artificial heart, pace-
 maker. Respiratory- artificial lungs. Excretory- Artificial kidney and skin. Artificial Support and
 replacement of human organs: artificial liver and pancreas. Total joint replacements- artificial limbs.
 Visual prosthesis -artificial / bionic eye.
  Course Outcomes: After completing the course, the students will be able to:
 CO1: Understand the fundamental concepts of biological systems, biometric principles, and struc-
   ture–function relationships to understand their relevance in engineering design and innovation.
 CO2: Apply bioinspired principles to develop engineering solutions for complex problems
 CO3: Evaluate the bioinspired materials for their advanced applications in the various domains.
 CO4: Analyze ethical, societal,and sustainability aspects of bioinspired engineering designs.

 RUBRICS FOR THE CONTINUOUS INTERNAL EVALUATION
    #                                      COMPONENTS                                            MARKS
    1       QUIZZES: Quizzes will be conducted in online/offline mode. TWO quizzes                  20

    2       TESTS: Students will be evaluated in test through descriptive questions with            40

            Understanding, Applying, Analyzing, Evaluating, and Creating). THREE
            tests will be conducted (Two regular tests and One optional Improvement

            Final test marks will be reduced to 40.

            tions. The evaluation shall be conducted in two phases. The implementation
            modalities for experiential learning activity shall be decided considering the
            nature of the course and its interdisciplinary requirements. Each phase shall
            carry 20 marks, with the total evaluation carrying 40 marks.

# RVCE Hostel Facilities and Accommodation

## Source and authority
Primary source: RVCE, *Hand Book for General Information*, 2025–26, pp. 43–44, “5.8 Hostel Facility.” Official PDF: https://rvce.edu.in/sites/default/files/Handbook_2025-26-28-08-2025-final.pdf

## General facilities
- RVCE’s handbook describes four boys’ hostels on campus and two ladies’ hostel locations: one on campus and one near the college.
- Hostel facilities listed in the handbook include a modern kitchen and dining hall, Wi-Fi/internet, common TV room, solar heating/heat-pump facility, and gymnasium.
- Each hostel has a residential supervisor. Boarders are encouraged to participate in the mess committee and in NSS, NCC, innovative club, cultural and sports activities.

## Discipline notice
The handbook states that violating college/hostel norms may lead to a student being prevented from continuing in that semester and/or appearing for semester examinations, depending on the Disciplinary Action Committee’s findings. It also states that ignorance of rules does not excuse misconduct.

## Retrieval guidance
For questions about capacity, occupancy, allocation by year, facilities or contacts, cite the 2025–26 handbook. Hostel assignment and contact details can change; tell users to verify current availability and contacts with RVCE. Do not silently resolve apparent capacity arithmetic discrepancies (for example, room count × occupancy differing from printed total capacity); preserve the printed value and flag the discrepancy if asked.

                                    Unit - I                                            9    Hours
 ENVIRONMENT AND ECOSYSTEM: Definition, scope and importance of environment; com-
 ponents of environment; need for environmental awareness. Ecosystem structure and functions,
 energy flow, ecological succession, biodiversity and ecosystem services. Natural resources, environ-
 mental degradation, climate change and global environmental issues.
                                   Unit - II                                            9    Hours
 NATURAL RESOURCES AND ENVIRONMENTAL MANAGEMENT : Water demand
 and conservation, rainwater harvesting and runoff estimation. Municipal solid waste, hazardous waste
 and e-waste management. Waste minimization techniques, landfill concepts and numerical problems
 on waste generation. Sustainable utilization of water, land and energy resources.
                                   Unit - III                                           9    Hours
 SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT AND CIRCULAR ECONOMY : Concept of develop-
 ment and sustainability; economic, social and environmental dimensions; challenges to sustainability.
 Millennium Development Goals (MDGs) and Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). Systems think-
 ing, circular economy, industrial ecology and life-cycle thinking. Applications to water, energy, food,
 land, forests and waste management.
                                   Unit - IV                                            9    Hours
 GREEN TECHNOLOGIES AND EMERGING TOOLS FOR SUSTAINABILITY : En-
 ergy management and conservation. Hydrogen, ocean, tidal and geothermal energy. Sustainable
 urbanization, green buildings and green materials. AI for climate modelling, biodiversity assessment,
 pollution monitoring using sensors and satellite data, AI and GIS for resource mapping, smart water
 management, air quality forecasting, disaster prediction and smart waste management.
                                   Unit - V                                             9    Hours
 ENVIRONMENTAL GOVERNANCE, SAFETY AND CSR : Environmental protection
 and environmental legislation. Environmental audit. Occupational Health and Safety Management
 Systems (OHSMS), ISO 45001:2018. Fire safety, prevention and firefighting systems. Corporate
 Social Responsibility (CSR), stakeholder management, sustainability reporting (GRI, DJSI, CEPI)
 and investor perspectives.
  Course Outcomes: After completing the course, the students will be able to:
 CO1: Explain the concepts of environment, ecosystems, natural resources and environmental man-
   agement practices for sustainable development.
 CO2: Analyze sustainability principles, circular economy concepts, SDGs and resource management
   strategies for addressing environmental challenges.
 CO3: Apply renewable energy technologies, green engineering practices and AI-enabled tools for
   environmental monitoring and sustainable resource utilization.
 CO4: Interpret environmental legislation, occupational health and safety requirements, corporate
   social responsibility and sustainability reporting frameworks for responsible environmental gov-
   ernance.

    #                                     COMPONENTS                                          MARKS

            modalities for experiential learning activity shall be decided considering the

            carry 20 marks, with the total evaluation carrying 40 marks.

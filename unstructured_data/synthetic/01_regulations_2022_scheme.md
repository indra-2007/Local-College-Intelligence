# Academic Regulations - 2022 Scheme

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Not a real institutional policy. Created only for testing a RAG pipeline.

## 1. Applicability
1.1 These regulations apply to all UG students admitted in academic years 2022-23 and 2023-24.

## 3. Attendance

### 3.1 Minimum attendance
A student must have a minimum of 75% attendance in each course (theory and laboratory alike) to be eligible to appear for the Semester End Examination (SEE) in that course.

### 3.2 Condonation of shortage
Shortage of attendance up to 10 percentage points (that is, attendance between 65% and 75%) may be condoned by the Principal on medical grounds, subject to a valid medical certificate. A condonation fee of Rs. 500 per course is payable.

### 3.3 Detention
A student with attendance below 65% in a course cannot be condoned. The student is detained in that course and must repeat it in a subsequent academic year.

## 4. Internal Assessment and Examinations

### 4.1 Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE)
CIE carries 50 marks: three internal tests are conducted, the best two are averaged and scaled to 30 marks, and assignments/quizzes carry 20 marks.

### 4.2 Minimum CIE for SEE eligibility
A student must score at least 20 out of 50 in CIE to be eligible for the SEE.

### 4.3 Semester End Examination (SEE)
SEE is conducted for 100 marks and scaled down to 50. A student must score at least 35 out of 100 in SEE, and at least 40 out of 100 overall (CIE + scaled SEE), to pass a course.

## 5. Revaluation
5.1 A student may apply for revaluation within 7 days of the publication of results.
5.2 The revaluation fee is Rs. 1000 per course.

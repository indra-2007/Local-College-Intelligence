- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 2.2 Entry and Exit
- Hostel gate entry/exit is logged via biometric scanner.
- Students leaving campus after 6:00 PM on weekdays must obtain a gate pass from the warden.
- Weekend outing: Students may leave campus on Saturday after classes and must return by Sunday 8:00 PM.
- Night stay outside campus requires written request to the warden with parent/guardian call confirmation.
- Visitors are allowed only in the common area during visiting hours (4:00 PM – 6:00 PM on weekdays, 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM on weekends).

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 2.3 Room Discipline
- Rooms must be kept clean. Room inspection every Wednesday by the hostel supervisor.
- Prohibited items: Electric heaters, hot plates, immersion rods, alcohol, tobacco products, inflammable substances.
- Permitted appliances: Personal laptop, table fan, phone charger, desk lamp.
- Any damage to room furniture or fittings will be charged to the student's caution deposit.
- Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate expulsion from the hostel and disciplinary action as per UGC anti-ragging regulations.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 2.4 Leave and Vacation
- Hostel residents must vacate within 3 days of the last examination of each semester.
- Students requiring vacation stay (for internships, projects, or placements) must apply to the warden at least 7 days in advance.
- Vacation stay charges: ₹200/day (includes mess charges).
- International students may apply for year-round accommodation with the International Students Office.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 3.2 Mess Committee
- A Mess Committee of 6 students (1 per block + 2 at large) is elected each semester.
- The committee meets monthly with the mess contractor and hostel warden to review quality, hygiene, and menu suggestions.
- Complaints may be filed via the mess suggestion box or the "Mess Feedback" form on the Student Portal.
- Hygiene inspection by the college medical officer: once every 2 weeks.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

## 4. Medical and Emergency

### 4.1 Hostel Sick Room
- Each hostel block has a sick room (2 beds) for minor ailments.

- Emergency ambulance tie-up with City General Hospital (5 km from campus). Response time: ~15 minutes.
- First-aid kits available with the hostel warden (ground floor office).

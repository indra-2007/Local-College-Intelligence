# Student Club Directory and Recruitment Guidelines - AY 2025-26

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Created only for testing a RAG pipeline.

## 1. Overview of Student Clubs

SIT recognises 18 official student clubs across 5 categories: Technical, Cultural, Literary, Sports, and Social Outreach. Each club operates under a faculty advisor and is governed by the Student Activity Council (SAC). All clubs must re-register with the Dean of Student Affairs at the beginning of each academic year.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

## 5. Recruitment Process

### 5.1 Recruitment Timeline
All club recruitments for the academic year follow this schedule:
- Week 1–2 of odd semester: Club fair and orientation (all clubs set up stalls in the quadrangle).
- Week 3: Application forms open online via the Student Portal.
- Week 4–5: Selection rounds (tests, auditions, interviews as applicable).
- Week 6: Final selection lists published on notice boards and Student Portal.
- Late joiners: Clubs may accept mid-semester applications at the club coordinator's discretion, subject to vacancy.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 5.2 Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum CGPA of 6.0 to hold any club office-bearer position (President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer).
- No active disciplinary proceedings against the student.
- Maximum 2 club memberships allowed per student to ensure academic balance.
- First-year students may join clubs but cannot hold office-bearer positions until 2nd year.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 5.3 Faculty Advisor Responsibilities
- Each club must have one designated faculty advisor approved by the Dean of Student Affairs.
- The advisor must attend at least one club meeting per month.
- All club expenditures above ₹5,000 require faculty advisor approval.
- Event proposals must be co-signed by the faculty advisor before submission to SAC.

- access: faculty, hod, admin

## 6. Budget and Funding

### 6.1 Annual Budget Allocation
- Each club receives a base allocation of ₹15,000/year from the Student Activity Fund.
- Technical clubs receive an additional ₹10,000 for equipment and components.
- Clubs may raise additional funds through sponsorships with prior SAC approval.
- All financial transactions must be documented and submitted to the SAC Treasurer by March 31.

- access: faculty, hod, admin

### 6.2 Sponsorship Guidelines
- Clubs may approach external sponsors for events with a minimum of 30 days advance notice to SAC.
- Sponsorship agreements must not include exclusivity clauses that conflict with institutional partnerships.
- All sponsor logos and branding must be approved by the Public Relations Office.
- Revenue from sponsorships is to be deposited in the club's official account within 7 working days.

- access: hod, admin

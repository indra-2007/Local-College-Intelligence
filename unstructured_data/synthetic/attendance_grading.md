# Attendance, grading and passing standards

## Attendance
Minimum attendance is 85% in each course. Principal may condone up to 10 percentage points for documented serious medical conditions or approved representation in sports, cultural, technical or academic activities. Documents and prior permission apply as specified by the handbook. 75% is mandatory even in these cases. Students who fail the requirement cannot attend SEE for that course. Condonation is not automatic.

## Absolute grading on 10-point scale
| Marks (%) | Grade | Points |
|---|---|---:|
| 90–100 | O | 10 |
| 80–89 | A+ | 9 |
| 70–79 | A | 8 |
| 60–69 | B+ | 7 |
| 55–59 | B | 6 |
| 50–54 | C | 5 |
| 40–49 | P | 4 |
| 0–39 | F | 0 |

SGPA = sum of credits × grade points divided by sum of credits for registered courses, excluding transitional grades. CGPA is cumulative across semesters.

## Passing requirements
Theory and lab/project: CIE at least 40%, SEE at least 35%, aggregate at least 40%. The handbook also gives combined theory + laboratory passing requirements: aggregate at least 60 marks, with separate CIE and SEE thresholds. For theory courses with 100 CIE marks, the quiz/test component minimum is 24/60 and full CIE minimum 40/100. For lab CIE 50 marks, minimum is 20/50. Refer to source for special cases.

# RVCE Hostel Information — Official Website Page (Separate Legacy Source)

## Source
RVCE official website, “RVCE-HOSTEL”: https://oldback.rvce.edu.in/?q=node%2F1065
Related official infrastructure page: https://oldback.rvce.edu.in/?q=infra

## Content reported by the page
- RVCE describes hostel accommodation as sharing-based, with room occupancy depending on semester and student year.
- The page describes one common mess in Cauvery block serving vegetarian food.
- Boys’ hostels are described as located on the RVCE campus. The page lists Chamundi and Cauvery Annex for first-year UG students, Cauvery for second- and third-year UG students, and Sir M. Visveswaraya for final-year UG and PG students.
- The girls’ hostel page identifies Krishna Garden in Pattanagere and the DJ Block on campus, and says rooms are sharing type.
- Items the page says students should bring: bedding, bucket/mug, and daily-use items.
- The page says heating coils, iron boxes and tape recorders are not allowed, and says students must follow the college dress code.
- Hostel Office number printed on this page: 080-6818-8256 / 8271 (as displayed; verify before use).

## Important source conflict / freshness rule
This page is a separate official web source but its hostel block assignments and contact details differ from RVCE’s 2025–26 handbook. It does not provide a clear publication date in the accessible page. Do not combine its block-to-year assignments or phone numbers with the handbook’s current-edition data. If asked about rules to follow or current allocations, prefer a newer RVCE circular/handbook or direct confirmation from the hostel office.

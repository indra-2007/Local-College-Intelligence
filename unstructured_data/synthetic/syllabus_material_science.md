                                   Unit - I                                            9   Hours
 Review of mechanical properties: Stress-strain diagram, elastic deformation, plastic deforma-
 tion, Yield point phenomena, hardness, impact energy, ductile fracture, brittle fracture.
 textbfFatigue and Creep of materials: Types of cyclic loading, S–N curve and fatigue properties,
 mechanism of fatigue failure, Factors affecting fatigue life, Creep curve and stages, Creep mecha-
 nisms.
                                   Unit - II                                           9   Hours
 Crystal imperfections and plastic deformation: Types of defects, dislocations in plastic de-
 formation, Solid solutions, Hume Rothery Rules, Critical resolved shear stress.//Strengthening
 mechanisms: Grain size strengthening, Solid solution strengthening, Strain (work) hardening, Pre-
 cipitation and dispersion strengthening
                                  Unit - III                                           9   Hours
 Phase Diagrams and Fe-C equilibrium diagram: Introduction to phase, phase diagrams, Gibbs
 phase rule, Lever rule, cooling curves, Isomorphous alloy system, Iron-Iron carbide phase diagram-
 Invariant reactions. Solidification: Nucleation, homogeneous nucleation, heterogeneous nucleation,
 crystal growth
                                  Unit - IV                                            9   Hours
 Heat treatment of ferrous materials: Annealing, normalizing, hardening, tempering. TTT and
 CCTDiagrams. Surface heat treatment processes: carburizing, nitriding, cyaniding, flame, and
 induction hardening. Defects in heat treatment.
                                   Unit - V                                            9   Hours
 Modern Semiconductor Processing: Introduction, Unit processes - oxidation, photolithography,
 etching and doping, process sequences, information flow, process organization, goals of semiconductor
 manufacturing.Heat treatment of Electronic Materials: Purpose of heat treatment in electronic
 materials, Annealing, baking, solder reflow, oxidation, diffusion, rapid thermal processing.
  Course Outcomes: After completing the course, the students will be able to:
 CO1: Determine the mechanical properties and deformation behaviour of engineering materials using
   crystal structure and stress–strain relationships.
 CO2: Apply the principles of strengthening mechanisms and solidification techniques to enhance the
   properties and performance of engineering materials.
 CO3: Analyze the effects of heat treatment process on microstructure, mechanical properties, and
   defects in ferrous materials.
 CO4: Evaluate the influence of semiconductor processing and thermal treatment techniques in semi-
   conductor device manufacturing.

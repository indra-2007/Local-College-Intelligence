# RVCE Anti-Ragging Rules and Reporting Context

## Primary source: 2025–26 handbook
RVCE, *Hand Book for General Information*, 2025–26, pp. 53–54, section 7.2 “Anti-Ragging Rules.” Official PDF: https://rvce.edu.in/sites/default/files/Handbook_2025-26-28-08-2025-final.pdf

## Rule and definition
- The handbook states that ragging is a criminal offence and students must not indulge in, or directly or indirectly support, ragging. It states that severe action will be initiated against those who do so.
- RVCE defines ragging as causing, inducing, compelling or forcing a student, including as a practical joke, to do something that undermines dignity, violates the person, exposes the student to ridicule, or prevents a lawful act through intimidation, wrongful confinement, injury, criminal force or threats of these acts.

## Consequences listed by RVCE
The handbook lists rustication from the college; a statement in bold on the university degree and conduct certificates; ineligibility for placement services, hostel facility, air/rail concessions, scholarships and passport/visa clearance; publication of photographs on notice boards and in local newspapers; and the enquiry committee’s decision as final. It also refers to applicable legal provisions. This is a summary of RVCE’s handbook text, not independent legal advice.

## RVCE official anti-ragging webpage
Official page: https://oldback.rvce.edu.in/?q=anti-ragging
The page says RVCE has anti-ragging committees and awareness measures, including information in the rules book/handbook, posters, first-year orientation and awareness programs. It also states that online anti-ragging undertakings are required from each student and parent/guardian and points to national undertaking portals. Since portal availability and regulatory process can change, use current RVCE instructions before completing an undertaking.

## Stale content excluded
The anti-ragging webpage includes an “Anti-ragging week” date for August 2023 and committee rosters explicitly labelled as duties assigned during 2017–19. These are historical and are not represented here as current dates or current committee membership. The webpage also contains old statutory references; refer to current official RVCE instructions and applicable current law for legal questions.

## Retrieval guidance
For immediate danger or reporting contacts, the included RVCE sources do not establish a current dedicated anti-ragging phone number. Do not invent one. Direct the student to a current RVCE official contact/channel or campus authority and mark that the provided sources do not identify a verified current reporting number.

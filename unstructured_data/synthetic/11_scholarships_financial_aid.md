# Scholarship, Financial Aid, and Fee Concession Schemes - AY 2025-26

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Created only for testing a RAG pipeline.

## 1. Merit Scholarships

### 1.1 Academic Excellence Scholarship
- Eligibility: CGPA ≥ 9.0 in the previous academic year. No backlogs.
- Award: 50% tuition fee waiver for the following year.
- Number of scholarships: Top 2 students per branch per year.
- Application: Automatic — no application needed. TPC announces results in August.
- Renewal: Must maintain CGPA ≥ 9.0 and have zero backlogs each semester.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 1.2 Branch Topper Award
- Eligibility: Highest CGPA in each branch at the end of each academic year.
- Award: ₹25,000 cash prize + certificate from the Principal.
- Presented during the Annual Day ceremony.
- In case of a tie, the award is shared equally.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 1.3 Sports Scholarship
- Eligibility: Students representing SIT at university, state, or national level sports events.
- Award: 25% tuition fee waiver + free hostel accommodation.
- Application: Submit sports certificates and recommendation from the Physical Education Director to the Dean of Students.
- Deadline: 30 September (odd semester), 28 February (even semester).
- Maximum 10 sports scholarships per year.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

## 2. Government Scholarships (External)

### 2.1 Post-Matric Scholarship for SC/ST Students
- Administered by: Karnataka Department of Social Welfare
- Eligibility: SC/ST category students with family income ≤ ₹2,50,000/year.
- Covers: Full tuition + maintenance allowance of ₹3,000/month for hostellers.
- Application: Via SSP (State Scholarship Portal) — ssp.karnataka.gov.in
- Documents required: Caste certificate, income certificate, Aadhaar, bank passbook, previous year marks card.
- College role: Verify and forward applications to the government portal by 31 October.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 2.2 Central Sector Scheme for College Students (CSSS)
- Administered by: Ministry of Education, Government of India
- Eligibility: Students from families with income ≤ ₹8,00,000/year. Must have scored ≥ 80th percentile in Class 12 board exams.
- Award: ₹10,000/year for 1st–3rd year, ₹20,000/year for 4th year.
- Application: Via National Scholarship Portal (NSP) — scholarships.gov.in
- Deadline: Typically October each year (check NSP for exact dates).

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 2.3 E-Grantz (OBC/Minority Scholarship)
- Administered by: Karnataka Backward Classes Welfare Department
- Eligibility: OBC/Minority category students with family income ≤ ₹2,50,000/year.
- Covers: Tuition fee reimbursement + ₹1,500/month for hostellers.
- Application: Via SSP Karnataka portal.
- Documents: Community certificate, income certificate, Aadhaar, bank details.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

## 3. Institutional Financial Aid

### 3.1 Fee Concession for Economically Weaker Students
- Eligibility: Family income ≤ ₹3,00,000/year. Must maintain CGPA ≥ 6.0.
- Award: 25% – 75% tuition fee concession (decided case-by-case by the Fee Concession Committee).
- Application: Submit the Financial Aid Application Form (available at Accounts Office) with income certificate, bank statement, and recommendation letter from class mentor.
- Deadline: Within 30 days of semester commencement.
- Committee meets in September (odd) and February (even) to decide awards.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 3.2 Sibling Discount
- If two or more siblings are enrolled simultaneously at SIT, the second sibling receives a 10% tuition discount.
- Applicable from the semester both siblings are enrolled.
- Apply at the Accounts Office with proof of relationship (Aadhaar/PAN of both students showing same parents).

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 3.3 Emergency Financial Assistance
- For students facing sudden financial hardship (medical emergency, loss of earning family member).
- One-time grant of up to ₹50,000.
- Apply directly to the Dean of Students with supporting documents.
- Decided within 7 working days. Funds disbursed from the Student Welfare Fund.
- Maximum 1 grant per student during the entire course.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

## 4. Education Loans

### 4.1 College-Facilitated Bank Loans
- SIT has tie-ups with State Bank of India, Canara Bank, and Bank of Baroda for education loans.
- Loan amount: Up to ₹10,00,000 (no collateral needed up to ₹7,50,000 under government guidelines).
- Interest rate: 8.5% – 10% (varies by bank and government subsidy eligibility).
- Moratorium period: Until 1 year after course completion or 6 months after getting a job (whichever is earlier).
- Apply at the respective bank branch with admission letter, fee structure, and income documents.
- College provides bonafide certificate and fee receipt for loan processing.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 4.2 Interest Subsidy (Central Government)
- Students from families with income ≤ ₹4,50,000/year are eligible for full interest subsidy during the moratorium period.
- Subsidy available for loans taken from scheduled banks only.
- Apply through the Vidya Lakshmi Portal — vidyalakshmi.co.in.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

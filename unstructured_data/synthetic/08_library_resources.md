# Library Rules, Digital Resources, and Study Spaces - AY 2025-26

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Created only for testing a RAG pipeline.

## 1. Library Timings and Access

### 1.1 Operating Hours
| Day | Timing |
|---|---|
| Monday – Friday | 8:00 AM – 9:00 PM |
| Saturday | 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM |
| Sunday | Closed (except during exam weeks: 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM) |
| Public holidays | Closed |

- Library access requires a valid college ID card. ID must be presented at the entry counter.
- Final year students preparing for placements may request extended access (up to 10:00 PM) with HoD recommendation.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 1.2 Borrowing Limits
| Category | Books | Duration | Renewal |
|---|---|---|---|
| UG Students | 3 books | 14 days | 1 renewal (14 days) |
| PG Students | 5 books | 21 days | 2 renewals (14 days each) |
| Faculty | 10 books | 90 days | Unlimited renewals |
| Research Scholars | 8 books | 30 days | 2 renewals |

- Overdue fine: ₹2 per day per book.
- Lost book: Replacement cost + ₹200 processing fee. If the book is out of print, 3× the original price.
- Reference books, journals, and periodicals are for in-library use only — not for borrowing.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

## 2. Digital Library Resources

### 2.1 Online Databases Available
- IEEE Xplore (full access to IEEE journals and conference papers)
- Springer Nature (selected engineering and science journals)
- NPTEL video lectures (integrated with college LMS)
- ACM Digital Library (computer science proceedings)
- ScienceDirect (Elsevier — selected titles)
- Shodhganga (Indian thesis repository — free access)

Remote access: Available via VPN. Students can download the VPN client from the IT Services page on the Student Portal. VPN credentials are the same as the portal login.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 2.2 Plagiarism Check
- Turnitin access is provided to all final-year UG and PG students for project/thesis work.
- Each student gets 5 submission attempts per semester.
- Faculty can request unlimited checks through the library portal.
- Similarity index must be below 25% for thesis submission (excluding bibliography and quotes).

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

## 3. Study Spaces and Facilities

### 3.1 Quiet Study Zones
- Main Reading Hall (Ground Floor): 120 seats, absolute silence required. No laptops — pen and paper only.
- Digital Study Room (First Floor): 40 seats with power outlets. Laptops and tablets permitted. Headphones mandatory for any audio.
- Exam Preparation Room (Second Floor): Available only during CIE and SEE exam periods. 60 seats. Booking via library portal (2-hour slots).

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 3.2 Group Discussion Rooms
- 4 enclosed rooms (capacity 6–8 persons each) on the First Floor.
- Booking: Via library portal, maximum 2 hours per booking, up to 1 booking per day per student.
- Equipped with whiteboard, markers, and wall-mounted display for presentations.
- Noise must be contained within the room. Doors must remain closed during use.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 3.3 Printing and Scanning
- Self-service printing kiosk near the circulation desk.
- Cost: ₹2 per page (B&W), ₹10 per page (colour).
- Scanning: Free (email to self or USB drive).
- Photocopying: ₹1 per page. Copyright notice: maximum 10% of any single book may be photocopied.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

## 4. Book Requisition

### 4.1 Requesting New Books
- Students and faculty may request new book purchases through the "Book Requisition" form on the library portal.
- Each department has an annual book budget of ₹50,000. HoD approval required for purchases above ₹2,000 per title.
- Requisitions are processed monthly. Expected procurement time: 30–45 days for Indian publications, 60–90 days for international.
- Textbooks prescribed in the syllabus are given priority.

- access: faculty, hod, admin

### 4.2 Book Bank Scheme
- Economically weaker students (family income < ₹5,00,000/year) may apply for the Book Bank scheme.
- Selected students receive one set of prescribed textbooks for the semester on returnable basis.
- Application period: First 2 weeks of each semester. Documents required: income certificate, Aadhaar, application form.
- Books must be returned by the last day of the semester. Non-return results in block on next semester registration.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

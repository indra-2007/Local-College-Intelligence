 Guidelines for Design Thinking Lab:
  1. The Design Thinking Lab (DTL) is to be carried out by a team of two–three students.
  2. Each student in a team must contribute equally to the tasks mentioned below.
  3. Each group has to select a theme that provides solutions to challenges of societal concern. Nor-
     mally, three to four themes will be identified by the department.
  4. Each group should follow the stages of Empathy, Design, Ideate, Prototype, and Test for successful
     completion of the DTL.
  5. After every stage of the DTL, the committee constituted by the department, along with the
     coordinators, will conduct the Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE). The committee shall consist
     of the respective coordinator and two senior faculty members as examiners. The evaluation will
     be carried out separately for each student.
  6. The team should prepare a digital poster, and a final report shall be submitted after incorporating
     the modifications, if any, suggested by the evaluation committee.
 The Design Thinking lab tasks would involve: 1. Carry out the detailed questionnaire
  7. Carry out a detailed questionnaire to identify the problem under the selected theme. Prepare an
     empathy report based on the responses obtained from the stakeholders.
  8. For the identified problem, the team shall propose an innovative, out-of-the-box solution to suc-
     cessfully complete the Ideation stage of the Design Thinking Lab (DTL).
  9. Once the solution idea is finalized, formulate a detailed design during the Design stage, considering
     its practical feasibility and implementation aspects.
 10. After approval of the design, implement the proposed solution and develop a working prototype
     of the system.
 11. Conduct thorough testing of all modules of the developed prototype and perform integrated
     testing to validate its overall functionality.
 12. Demonstrate the functioning of the prototype along with a presentation explaining the design,
     implementation, and testing outcomes.
 13. Prepare a digital poster illustrating each stage of the Design Thinking Lab (DTL). Submit a
     detailed project report highlighting the activities carried out, along with the difficulties and
     challenges encountered at every stage of the DTL.
  Course Outcomes: After completing the course, the students will be able to:
 CO1: Apply design thinking principles to identify and define societal problems through stakeholder
   engagement and need analysis.
 CO2: Analyze user requirements and generate innovative solutions using creative problem-solving
   techniques.
 CO3: Evaluate design alternatives through prototyping, testing, validation, and performance assess-
   ment.

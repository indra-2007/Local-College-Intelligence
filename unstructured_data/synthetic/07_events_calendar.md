## 4. Event Organisation Guidelines

### 4.1 Event Proposal Process
1. Club coordinator submits Event Proposal Form (EPF) to SAC at least 21 days before the event.
2. EPF must include: event description, date/time, venue, expected attendance, budget estimate, faculty advisor signature.
3. SAC reviews within 5 working days and may request modifications.
4. Approved events are listed on the campus events calendar and announced via Student Portal.
5. Venue booking is done through the Estate Office after SAC approval — clubs cannot book venues directly.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 4.2 Attendance and Academic Impact
- Students participating in SAC-approved events may request attendance exemption for the event days.
- Exemption request must be submitted by the club coordinator to the respective HoD at least 7 days in advance.
- Maximum 5 days of event-related attendance exemption per semester per student.
- Exemption does not apply to CIE test days or lab examination days.
- Students with attendance below 65% are not eligible for event attendance exemption.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 4.3 Safety and Logistics
- All events ending after 7:00 PM must have security arrangements approved by the Chief Security Officer.
- Events involving external participants require a participant register with ID proof.
- Fire safety clearance required for indoor events with expected attendance above 200.
- First-aid station mandatory for all sports events and events lasting more than 4 hours.
- Campus cafeteria extends hours during major fests (subject to prior arrangement).

- access: faculty, hod, admin

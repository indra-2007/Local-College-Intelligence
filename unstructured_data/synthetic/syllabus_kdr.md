                                   Unit - I                                            9   Hours
 Introduction to information retrieval and architecture of a search engine: Search Engines
 and Information Retrieval–What Is Information Retrieval? The Big Issues, Search Engines, Search
 Engineers.
 Architecture of a Search Engine: What is architecture? Basic Building Blocks, Breaking It
 Down.
                                   Unit - II                                           9   Hours
 Crawls and Feeds: Deciding what to search, Crawling the Web, Crawling Documents and Email,
 Document Feeds, The Conversion Problem, Storing the Documents, Detecting Duplicates, Removing
 Noise.
 Processing Text: From words to text, Text Statistics, Document Parsing, Document Structure
 and Markup, Link Analysis, Information Extraction.
                                  Unit - III                                           9   Hours
 Ranking with Indexes: Overview, Abstract Model of Ranking, Inverted indexes, Compression,
 Auxiliary Structures, Index Construction, Query Processing.
                                  Unit - IV                                            9   Hours
 Queries and Interfaces: Information Needs and Queries, Query Transformation and Refinement,
 Showing the Results, Cross-Language Search.
                                   Unit - V                                            9   Hours
 Retrieval Models: Overview of Retrieval Models, Probabilistic Models, Ranking Based on Lan-
 guage Models. Evaluating Search Engines: Why Evaluate? The Evaluation Corpus, Effectiveness
 Metrics, Efficiency Metrics.
  Course Outcomes: After completing the course, the students will be able to:
 CO1: Apply fundamental principles of Information Retrieval principles to extract relevant information
   from the given problem.
 CO2: Analyze the different Information Retrieval techniques, retrieval models and search engines
   appropriate for a given problem by engaging in lifelong learning for emerging technology.
 CO3: Evaluate the effectiveness of Information Retrieval systems using different metrics on different
   case studies.
 CO4: Explore advanced topics such as web search, relevance feedback, and multimedia information
   retrieval for real-world applications through effective communication.
 CO5: Demonstrate solutions using concepts of Information Retrieval by exhibiting team work and
   effective communication.

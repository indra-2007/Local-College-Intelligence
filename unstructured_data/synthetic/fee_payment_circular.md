# Higher semester fee payment circular

For higher semester UG and PG students for AY 2026–27. SAP Student Portal payment was proposed to open from 7 September 2026; confirmation was to be issued separately. Students must pay within 15 days of actual portal opening. Prefer online payment using existing SAP student login; online payment generates an immediate receipt. Outstanding dues may prevent hall-ticket download or issuance of academic certificates; late payment attracts a penalty, amount not specified. Verify applicable amounts in SAP. The circular is available under Circulars & Notifications > Fee Circulars and in the individual SAP portal under Circulars. No confirmed opening date or exact deadline is supplied.

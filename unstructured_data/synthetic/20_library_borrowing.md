# Library borrowing and working hours

UG students can borrow four books; PG students five. SC/ST students receive two additional books. Loan period is two weeks, with one renewal for another two weeks. Late fee ₹1 per day. Reference books must be read within library premises. Reservation is available. No-dues clearance requires return of all borrowed documents and settlement of outstanding dues. Duplicate smart ID card fee ₹1000: collect challan from Admin Block cashier, pay at Kotak Bank and submit receipt to library.

| Day | Working hours |
|---|---|
| Monday–Friday | 09:00–21:00 |
| Saturday (2nd and 4th working Saturdays) | 09:00–17:00 |
| Sunday | 09:00–13:30 |

Digital library internet and e-resources are available to students subject to rules. Contacts: library@rvce.edu.in; librarian@rvce.edu.in.

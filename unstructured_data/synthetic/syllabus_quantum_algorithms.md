                                 Unit - III                                          9   Hours
 Advanced Quantum Algorithms: Variational Quantum Algorithms, Quantum Approximate Op-

                                 Unit - IV                                           9   Hours
 Quantum Cryptography and Key Distribution: Introduction to Quantum Cryptography, Fun-
 damental QKD Protocols, QKD System Components, BB84 Quantum Key Distribution, detecting
 an Eavesdropper, Entanglement based QKD, Quantum Key Distribution Schemes Quantum Tic-Tac-
 Toe.
                                 Unit - V                                            9   Hours
 Case Studies and Emerging Trends in QKD: QKD in Smart grids, QKD in Cloud and IoT Secu-
 rity, QKD in Communication Networks, Real-World QKD Implementations, Industrial Deployments,
 Research Trends in QKD.
  Course Outcomes: After completing the course, the students will be able to:
 CO1: Analyze the computational principles of quantum algorithms, quantum parallelism, complexity
   measures, and fundamental quantum algorithmic paradigms to differentiate quantum computation
   from classical computation.
 CO2: Evaluate the design and computational efficiency of quantum algorithms including Deutsch-
   Jozsa, Bernstein-Vazirani, Simon’s, and Shor’s algorithms for solving computationally complex
   problems.
 CO3: Design optimized quantum computational solutions using variational quantum algorithms,
   quantum optimization methods, compiler optimizations, quantum architectures, and quantum
   error correction techniques
 CO4: Analyze quantum cryptographic principles and quantum key distribution protocols to evaluate
   their security against quantum attacks and eavesdropping.
 CO5: Assess emerging quantum key distribution technologies and real-world applications in com-
   munication networks, cloud computing, IoT, and smart grids to recommend secure quantum
   communication solutions.

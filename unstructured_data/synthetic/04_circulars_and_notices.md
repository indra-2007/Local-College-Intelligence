## Notice N4 - Attendance Shortage Intervention (Restricted)
- circular_no: SIT/ACAD/2025/031
- date: 2025-03-20
- access: hod, admin

Department HODs are to identify students whose attendance in any course is below 70% as of 15 March 2025 and arrange counselling through faculty mentors before 31 March 2025. The list of such students is confidential and must not be circulated to students or displayed on notice boards.

## Notice N5 - NBA Documentation Deadlines

- date: 2025-01-22
- access: faculty, hod, admin

Course coordinators must complete CO-PO mapping and attainment sheets for all courses by 15 March 2025. The department will compile the Self Assessment Report (SAR) evidence folders by 10 April 2025. Courses with missing CO-PO mapping will be flagged to the HOD.

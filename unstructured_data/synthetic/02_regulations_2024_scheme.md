# Academic Regulations - 2024 Scheme

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Not a real institutional policy. Created only for testing a RAG pipeline.
> This scheme deliberately differs from the 2022 Scheme in several clauses to test conflict handling.

## 1. Applicability
1.1 These regulations apply to all UG students admitted from academic year 2024-25 onwards.
1.2 Students admitted earlier continue to be governed by the 2022 Scheme.

## 3. Attendance

### 3.1 Minimum attendance
A student must have a minimum of 75% attendance in each theory course and a minimum of 85% attendance in each laboratory course to be eligible to appear for the Semester End Examination (SEE).

### 3.2 Condonation of shortage
Shortage of attendance in a theory course up to 8 percentage points (that is, attendance between 67% and 75%) may be condoned. The request must be recommended by the Head of the Department and approved by the Principal. A condonation fee of Rs. 750 per course is payable. A student may avail condonation at most twice during the entire programme.

### 3.3 Laboratory courses
Attendance shortage in laboratory courses cannot be condoned.

### 3.4 Detention
A student with attendance below 67% in a theory course, or below the 85% minimum in a laboratory course, is detained in that course and must repeat it.

## 4. Internal Assessment and Examinations

### 4.1 Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE)
CIE carries 50 marks: two internal tests of 30 marks each are conducted and averaged to 30 marks, and continuous assessment (assignments, quizzes, mini-projects) carries 20 marks.

### 4.2 Minimum CIE for SEE eligibility
A student must score at least 25 out of 50 in CIE to be eligible for the SEE.

### 4.3 Semester End Examination (SEE)
SEE is conducted for 100 marks and scaled down to 50. A student must score at least 35 out of 100 in SEE, and at least 40 out of 100 overall (CIE + scaled SEE), to pass a course.

## 5. Revaluation
5.1 A student may apply for revaluation within 5 days of the publication of results.
5.2 The revaluation fee is Rs. 1200 per course.

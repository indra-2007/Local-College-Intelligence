 Unit-I
 Introduction to NCC and Indian Armed Forces – Introduction to NCC, Aims, organization,
 and significance of NCC, Overview of the Indian Armed Forces, Organizational structure, Ranks,
 Entry into the Indian Army.
 Unit-II
 Drill- Foot Drill, Drill ki Aam Hidayaten, Word ki Command, Savdhan, Vishram, Aram Se, Murdna,
 KadvarSizing, Teen Line Banana, Khuli Line, Nikat Line, Khade Khade Salute Karna.
 Unit-III
 Weapon Training, Trekking, Map Reading and JD- Overview of weapons, Introduction to
 SLR (Self Loading Rifle), Introduction to trekking, Types of maps, Finding North, Determining own
 position, Knots, Judging distance
 Unit-IV
 Social Service and Disaster Management- Social Service and Community Development, Disaster
 management, Assistance during natural disasters, Fire prevention, Fire-fighting equipment.
  Course Outcomes: After completing the course, the students will be able to:
 CO1: Understand the aims, organization, and significance of the National Cadet Corps (NCC) and
   gain a basic overview of the Indian Armed Forces, including their structure, ranks, and entry
   options.
 CO2: Demonstrate proficiency in foot drill commands and movements, thereby developing discipline,
   coordination, and the ability to function effectively as part of a group.
 CO3: Understand the fundamentals of weapon training, trekking, map reading, and judging distance,
   including safe handling of the Self-Loading Rifle (SLR) and basic fieldcraft skills essential to the
   army way of life.
 CO4: Apply knowledge of social service, community development, and disaster management by par-
   ticipating in activities related to public welfare, disaster response, fire prevention, and emergency
   assistance.

 Unit-I
 Foundations of Dance and Music:  Introduction to dance as an art form and medium of
 expression  Role of dance in physical, mental, and emotional well-being  Body awareness: posture,
 alignment, flexibility, and balance  Warm-up, stretching, and cool-down techniques for dancers
  Understanding music fundamentals: beats, rhythm, tempo, and counts  Coordination of basic
 movements with music
 Unit-II
 Dance Styles and Skill Development:  Overview of major dance forms: Classical, Folk/Eastern,
 and Western  Basic steps, footwork, and expressions in different dance styles  Cultural and stylistic
 features of various dance forms  Assessment of student interest and aptitude through interaction 
 Grouping of students based on selected dance styles  Style-specific movement practice and improvi-
 sation
 Unit-III
 Choreography and Performance:  Advanced techniques in selected dance styles  Expert ses-
 sions and guest workshops in respective dance forms  Components of performance: space, energy,
 timing, and expression  Introduction to choreographies and structured dance routines  Basics of
 choreography: formations, transitions, and storytelling  Creation and performance of solo or group
 choreography
  Course Outcomes: After completing the course, the students will be able to:
 CO1: Understand the fundamentals of dance including body awareness, warm-up techniques, posture,
   and basic music elements such as rhythm, tempo, and counts
 CO2: Apply basic movements and techniques from classical, folk/eastern, and western dance forms,
   and demonstrate adaptability through improvisation and style-based practice.
 CO3: Analyze and interpret music to identify beats, rhythm, and phrasing, and execute chore-
   ographed movements with appropriate musicality and coordination
 CO4: Create and perform solo or group choreographies, demonstrating performance skills, stage pres-
   ence, teamwork, and confidence in a live or showcase setting

 Prerequisites
 Although there are no prerequisite qualifications for this subject, students must have a basic under-
 standing of and interest in the fields of art and design in order to enroll in it.
 Innovative Experiments
 Students must turn in assignments for each of the above said topics on a weekly basis and have
 to compulsorily take part in the museum visit. CIE will be evaluated based on a still life piece, a
 composition using any one of the media of composition and a presentation on Indian art styles and
 creation of a piece pertaining to the presented art style.
  1. Use points, line and curves to create various shapes and forms
  2. Use of shapes and forms to create various objects and structures
  3. Recognizing distinctions in objects when viewed from various perspectives and grasping basic
     notions of perspective
  4. Students will be introduced to the significance of color in art, as well as the principles of color

  5. Applied the concepts of unity, harmony, balance, rhythm, emphasis and proportion, abstraction

## 2. Eligibility for Campus Placements

### 2.1 General Eligibility
- No active backlogs at the time of sitting for placement drives.
- Minimum CGPA of 6.0 (some companies may set a higher cutoff — this will be specified in the Job Description).
- Minimum 75% attendance in the current semester.
- Must have registered on the Placement Portal and completed the profile (resume, skills, certifications).
- Students with disciplinary action on record are not eligible for campus placements.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 2.2 Dream and Super Dream Policy
- Students who receive an offer below ₹5 LPA (CTC) are eligible to sit for all subsequent companies.
- Students who receive an offer between ₹5 LPA – ₹10 LPA (Dream) can sit only for Super Dream companies (₹10 LPA+).
- Students who receive a Super Dream offer (₹10 LPA+) are removed from the placement process.
- Students may voluntarily opt out of a received offer (in writing to TPC) to continue sitting for other companies. However, this is discouraged and counts as a "slot used."

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 2.3 Maximum Offers Rule
- Each student may hold a maximum of 2 offers at any time.
- Upon receiving a 2nd offer, the student must accept one and release the other within 48 hours.
- Failure to respond within 48 hours results in TPC making the decision on the student's behalf.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

## 3. Placement Drive Process

### 3.1 Pre-Placement Talk (PPT)
- Every visiting company conducts a PPT 1–2 days before the recruitment drive.
- Attendance at the PPT is mandatory if you intend to sit for that company.
- PPT covers: company overview, job roles, compensation, work culture, and eligibility criteria.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 3.2 Typical Recruitment Rounds
| Round | Description | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Online Test | Aptitude + Technical MCQs + Coding (on HackerRank/Mettl) | 60–90 min |
| Technical Interview (Round 1) | DSA, DBMS, OS, CN, project discussion | 30–45 min |
| Technical Interview (Round 2) | Deep dive into projects, system design (for senior roles) | 30–45 min |
| HR Interview | Behavioural questions, salary discussion, joining date | 15–30 min |

- Not all companies conduct all rounds. Some fast-track with only 2 rounds.
- Results are announced within 1–7 days of the drive. TPC communicates via email and the Placement Portal.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 3.3 Dress Code for Placement Drives
- Formal attire mandatory: Men — shirt, trousers, formal shoes; Women — formal kurta/shirt, trousers/formal skirt.
- No jeans, sneakers, or casual wear.
- College ID card must be worn.
- Carry: 3 copies of resume, mark sheets (all semesters), ID proof, passport-size photos.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

## 4. Internship Programme

### 4.1 Mandatory Internship (7th/8th Semester)
- All students must complete a minimum 4-week internship before the final semester.
- Internships may be sourced through TPC or self-sourced (with TPC approval).
- Internship report must be submitted to the department within 15 days of completion.
- Viva voce on the internship is part of the final semester evaluation (2 credits).
- Stipend is not mandatory but most TPC-sourced internships offer ₹10,000 – ₹30,000/month.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 4.2 Summer Internship (Optional)
- TPC facilitates summer internship opportunities (May–July) for 4th and 6th semester students.
- Students interested must register on the Placement Portal by 1 March.
- Selected students receive a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the college.
- Companies offering PPOs (Pre-Placement Offers) through internships are given priority in the placement calendar.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

## 5. Training Calendar

### 5.1 Pre-Placement Training Modules
| Module | Semester | Duration | Topic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aptitude Foundation | 4th sem | 30 hours | Quantitative ability, logical reasoning, verbal ability |
| Technical Foundations | 5th sem | 40 hours | DSA, DBMS, OS, CN fundamentals |
| Coding Bootcamp | 6th sem | 30 hours | Problem solving on LeetCode/HackerRank, contest practice |
| Soft Skills & GD | 7th sem | 20 hours | Group discussion, communication, email etiquette |
| Mock Interviews | 7th sem | 10 sessions | 1-on-1 mock technical + HR interviews with feedback |

- Training sessions are conducted on Saturdays (no regular class conflict).
- Attendance in training is tracked separately. Minimum 80% attendance required to sit for campus drives.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 5.2 Certifications Encouraged
- AWS Cloud Practitioner
- Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate
- NPTEL domain elective certification (counts for 2 credits if score ≥ 60%)
- Cisco CCNA (for networking-track students)
- Microsoft Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900)

TPC arranges discounted group registrations for these certifications. Approach the Placement Cell for current offers.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

## 6. Code of Conduct

### 6.1 Student Obligations
- Once a student confirms participation in a drive, they must attend all rounds. No-shows are penalized with a 1-company ban.
- Accepting an offer is binding. Reneging on an accepted offer results in debarment from all future campus drives.
- Students must not share interview questions or test content on social media or with other candidates during the drive.
- Any malpractice during online tests (plagiarism, proxy attendance) results in permanent debarment.

- access: student, faculty, hod, admin

### 6.2 Grievance Mechanism
- Placement-related grievances may be submitted in writing to the Placement Officer.
- A Placement Grievance Committee (PGC) comprising the Placement Officer, 2 HoDs, and 2 student representatives reviews complaints within 7 working days.
- Decisions of the PGC are final and binding.

- access: faculty, hod, admin

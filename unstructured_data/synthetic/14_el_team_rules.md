# Semester III experiential learning team formation

Applies to semester III students of the 2025 admission batch, 2025 Scheme. Teams comprise 4–5 students from at least two programmes within the same cluster. Normally no more than three students from one programme, except specifically approved combinations. Only one representative submits the team on Quiklrn; options close when the permitted quota is reached. Incorrect or nonconforming teams may be modified or cancelled.

## EL project and evaluation
Select one theme within the cluster and define measurable, achievable objectives. Group EL applies to serial numbers 1, 3, 4, 5 and 6 of the general semester III scheme; remaining courses use individual EL as applicable. Evaluation is by presentations and exhibition/display mode as applicable. Expected outcomes include one or more of peer-reviewed journal publication, product development and patent filing/patenting. Dates are in the academic calendar.

## CS cluster themes
Artificial Intelligence; Internet of Things; AR, VR & MR; Quantum Security; Edge Computing; Ethical AI; Cloud Security; Programming Mechanics (Coding for App Development); AI for Social Good; AI in Education; Data Science and Analytics.
